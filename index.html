Preserving the Home Front, Securing the Future.

For over six decades, the Police Officers’ Wives Association (POWA) has served as an essential pillar of stability within the Nigeria Police Force barracks. What began as an internal support network has grown into a structured, nationwide social protection agency dedicated to healthcare access, youth scholarship funding, and women-focused micro-investment pipelines.

Message From our President

Speech By Mrs. Olufunmilola Mutiat Disu 23rd National President, Police Officers’ Wives Association (POWA)

I stand before you with a heart overflowing with gratitude and a deep sense of responsibility. As I assume the leadership of this noble association, I am reminded that POWA is much more than a social body; we are the backbone behind the badge and the silent strength behind the uniform. Our mission for 2026 and beyond is clear: we are moving from intention to alliance. Through our POWA CARES initiative, we are institutionalizing compassion. We are not just providing one-off donations; we are building a sustainable architecture of support that ensures every police wife is economically empowered, every police child is academically supported, and every widow is never left to walk alone. To my sisters in the barracks across all 36 states and the FCT, I say this: your resilience is our inspiration. Whether it is through our nationwide healthcare outreaches, our vocational training centers, or our nursing scholarship funds, this administration is committed to your total well-being. The police officer protects the nation, but the police officer’s wife preserves the home. Together, through unity and faith, we are securing the future of the Nigeria Police Force family. Join us as we work to make a measurable impact, one family at a time.

How it works in 4 easy steps

Grassroots Identification

State Command Chairpersons work directly with barracks community leaders to identify vulnerable widows, low-cadre police families, and exceptional youth facing immediate financial or medical hardships.

Needs Assessment

The National Secretariat vets entries against our 2026 framework guidelines, matching candidates to targeted paths whether immediate healthcare screening, educational school funding, or trade training.

Disbursal & Training

Beneficiaries receive direct assistance during state-wide command tours, ranging from prescription lenses and clinical care to ₦50,000 micro grants alongside intensive business-literacy workshops.

Monitoring & Impact

We track business growth metrics, vocational output, and school enrollment data over time, ensuring our structural social protection framework successfully transitions families into long-term self-sufficiency.
