Programs & Initiatives

Our Strategic Impact Pillars

Through targeted interventions and national advocacy, we deliver structured social protection models that directly elevate women, youth, and vulnerable households across Nigeria's police commands.

Work with us? Contact us now
POWA CARES Outreach

Our flagship social protection framework delivering free clinical health screenings, optical treatments, and vital livelihood support packages directly to low-cadre police families during nationwide command tours.

Vocational Training

Empowering women and widows within the barracks through practical skill-acquisition bootcamps, spanning modern tailoring, catering, sustainable agro-processing, and financial literacy.

Next-Gen Tech Literacy

Bridging the digital divide for barracks youth by introducing foundational software development tracks, computational logic workshops, and essential computing tool access.

Healthcare Advocacy

Hosting focused clinical awareness campaigns and health seminars addressing critical maternal health indicators, preventive diagnostic education, and fibroid screening setups.

Micro Investment Grants

Providing small-scale business owners and enterprising barracks women with interest-free seed capital and micro-investment micro-grants to establish long-term economic self-reliance.

Education Support

Funding academic scholarship pipelines for exceptional, low-income barracks children and providing material educational tool kits to support regional police schools.

Our Strategic Alliances & Partners

Sustaining a national social protection framework requires strong, transparent collaboration. POWA proudly aligns with government institutions, healthcare organizations, and corporate foundations to turn structural support plans into reality across every command.
